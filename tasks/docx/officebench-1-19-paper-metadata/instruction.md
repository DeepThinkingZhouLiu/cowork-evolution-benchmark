# OS-Copilot research intake and evidence review

Research Operations needs a source-bounded Word intake dossier for `paper.pdf` in `/app/input_files`. The frozen PDF is only one page, so the dossier must distinguish what is visible from what cannot yet be reviewed.

Deliver only `/app/output/os_copilot_research_intake.docx`. Keep every element editable; do not flatten the page into a screenshot or add PDF/XLSX/PPTX deliverables.

Create a professional document that:

- captures the exact displayed title, all authors in displayed order, symbol-based affiliation mapping, equal-contribution note, listed contact, project URL, arXiv identity/date/category, and relevant PDF property/accessibility gaps;
- turns the abstract into a structured review of problem, gap, proposed framework, named agent, operating-system coverage, reported GAIA result, Excel/PowerPoint learning claim, and claimed significance;
- reconstructs all three Figure 1 scenarios and explains how the caption says to read initial/final states, subtasks, and highlighted changes;
- includes a 12-row evidence control matrix with source locators and an internal link from each control ID to its evidence-register entry;
- includes an intake limitations/follow-up table. Explicitly prevent conclusions that the one-page source cannot support, including an invented DOI, venue, publisher, corresponding-author designation, GAIA baseline, confidence interval, or technical details from an unseen full paper;
- uses native Word heading styles, editable tables, a TOC field, bookmarks/internal hyperlinks, a landscape matrix section, distinct section headers, PAGE/NUMPAGES fields, repeating table headers, and non-splitting rows;
- adds three native reviewer comments anchored to the equal-contribution boundary, the qualified 35% result, and the one-page evidence boundary.

Use only the frozen PDF. A claim may be summarized, but names, order, mappings, figures, metrics, dates, scope and evidence gaps must remain exact.
